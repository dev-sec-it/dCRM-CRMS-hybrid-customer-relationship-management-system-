## 📌 Product Overview

**dCR - CRMS** is a complete, unified enterprise resource planning and customer relationship management suite designed for growing businesses, enterprises, retail chains, educational institutions, hospitality providers, and service agencies. 

It provides an end-to-end operational operating system for your business: managing your client relationships, sales pipelines, accounting invoices, physical inventory, workforce payroll, project task boards, hotel reservations, and student admissions in one seamless, high-speed desktop interface.

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Launch Application
- If using **`setup.exe`**: Complete the 3-step installer wizard and check *"Launch dCR - CRMS now"*.
- If using **`portable-dCRM-CRMS.exe`**: Double-click the file to open the software instantly.

### Step 2: Initial Setup & Workspace Configuration
- On first launch, choose your preferred **Industry Preset** (General Trading, Retail & POS, Education, Hospitality, or Services) to configure relevant fields and workflows.
- Set up your company profile, currency, and business preferences to begin managing your operations immediately.

## 🏢 Core Modules & Features

### 1. CRM & Customer Pipeline
- **Contact & Organization Directory**: Store complete profiles of leads, buyers, vendors, and business partners with phone numbers, billing addresses, and interaction histories.
- **Visual Sales Pipeline**: Interactive Kanban pipeline board to advance opportunities from Prospecting to Qualification, Proposal, Negotiation, and Closed-Won.
- **Deal Tracking**: Monitor expected close dates, weighted revenue forecasts, and stage progression rates.
- **Activity Timeline**: Log client calls, email notes, on-site meeting logs, and follow-up reminders.

### 2. Invoicing, Billing & Quotations
- **Professional Quotations & Estimates**: Generate itemized price quotations with discounts, terms of service, and expiry dates. Convert approved quotes into invoices with a single click.
- **GST & Tax Compliant Invoicing**: Automated calculation of CGST, SGST, IGST, or flat regional sales taxes with automated invoice numbering schemes.
- **Payment Collection & Receipts**: Record partial payments, bank wire references, cash receipts, and outstanding invoice aging summaries.
- **Printable Statements**: Export print-ready PDF invoices, debit notes, and customer account ledgers.

### 3. Inventory & Stock Management
- **Catalog & SKU Registry**: Manage products, serial numbers, barcodes, purchase prices, selling prices, and category hierarchies.
- **Real-Time Stock Tracking**: Automated stock deduction upon invoice generation and automated replenishment on purchase orders.
- **Low Stock Intelligence**: Automated alert indicators for products falling below safety threshold quantities.
- **Multi-Warehouse Allocation**: Track inventory across physical store shelves, central warehouses, and transit depots.

### 4. Projects & Task Boards
- **Kanban Task Management**: Drag-and-drop task boards categorized by backlog, in-progress, code/quality review, and completed.
- **Milestone & Sprint Tracking**: Group deliverables into measurable project milestones with progress completion bars.
- **Employee Assignment**: Assign tasks to designated team members with priority badges and deadline schedules.
- **Timesheets & Billable Hours**: Record worked employee hours per project for client billing and operational auditing.

### 5. HRMS & Workforce Management
- **Employee Master Directory**: Maintain full employment records, job designations, departmental allocations, and joining dates.
- **Daily Attendance Sheet**: Real-time logging of present, absent, half-day, and late attendance statuses.
- **Leave Request Workflow**: Structured leave submission and manager approval system for sick, casual, and annual leaves.
- **Payroll & Salary Slips**: Automated generation of monthly payroll breakdowns, allowances, deductions, and downloadable salary slips.

### 6. Service & Helpdesk Management
- **Customer Support Tickets**: Log incoming client complaints, warranty requests, and equipment maintenance tickets.
- **SLA & Priority Escalation**: Assign Low, Medium, High, or Urgent severity levels with target response timeframes.
- **Resolution History**: Maintain an internal knowledge base of customer issue resolutions for recurring troubleshooting.

### 7. Education & Academic Suite
- **Student Information System**: Comprehensive records of enrolled students, guardian contacts, roll numbers, and academic batches.
- **Admissions Processing**: Structured onboarding workflow from admission inquiry to application review and final enrollment.
- **Tuition Fee Tracking**: Automated generation of semester/monthly fee dues and installment receipts.

### 8. Hospitality & Hotel Management
- **Room Booking & Reservation Desk**: Visual room status grid showing occupied, available, reserved, and cleaning states.
- **Guest Check-In / Check-Out**: Rapid guest registration with identity verification and folio billing.
- **Restaurant & Room Service POS**: Integrated dining orders and room service charges billed directly to guest folios.

### 9. Industry Preset Selector
Switch the workspace layout and active terminology with a single click:
- **General Business & Trading**
- **Retail & Point of Sale**
- **Educational Institutes & Coaching Centers**
- **Hotels, Resorts & Restaurants**
- **Professional Services & IT Agencies**
- **Construction & Contracting**
- **Healthcare & Clinical Clinics**

### 10. Financial Reports & Business Intelligence
- **Revenue Summaries**: Periodical breakdown of gross revenue, collections, and outstanding receivables across 7-day, 30-day, 90-day, and yearly horizons.
- **Sales Conversion Analytics**: Conversion ratios from initial lead capture to signed contract.
- **Audit Trails**: Time-stamped activity logs tracking record creation, modification, and user actions.

### 11. Data Sovereignty, Backup & Recovery
- **One-Click Encrypted Backups**: Save your complete business database into a password-protected `.dcrm` encrypted backup file.
- **Instant Restoration**: Restore your entire workspace onto any workstation instantly.
- **Zero Lock-In**: Store your backup files on local disks, network storage, or personal cloud drives under your full control.

## 🔒 Data Privacy & Business Security

- **Strict Data Confidentiality**: Your customer lists, pricing strategies, employee salaries, and financial ledgers belong exclusively to you. In Offline mode, no business data is ever uploaded to external servers.
- **Role-Based Access Control**: Assign granular permissions to ensure sales staff only view leads, accountants only view invoices, and managers have administrative oversight.
- **Encrypted Local Storage**: Local databases and backup files are cryptographically protected against unauthorized external access.
